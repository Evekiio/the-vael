Privacy, simply.

This listening hub has no advertising pixels, analytics scripts, sign-up forms or embedded players. It does not set cookies or store listening preferences.

Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub may process connection information, including IP addresses, for security and operation of its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Listening links

Links take you to services such as YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music and social platforms. Those services have their own privacy practices. We load no third-party players or fonts before you choose a destination.

Sharing and contact

The Share button uses your device’s share sheet or copies this site’s address to your clipboard after you press it. Contact links open your email app. This site does not collect or submit a message.
